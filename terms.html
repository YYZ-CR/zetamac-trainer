This is a free arithmetic trainer. Using it means accepting the short list below. It is written to be read rather than to be impressive, and it is not a substitute for a lawyer neither of us has.

What you get

A timed arithmetic drill, an analysis of the run afterward, practice mode, a daily puzzle, duels, private clans and a profile page. All of it is free. There is nothing to buy, no paid tier, and no plan to add one that would take away something that works today.

Playing needs no account. An account exists so that your history, the leaderboards and your profile can be attached to a person.

It is still in beta, and scores may be reset

Say it plainly: scores, leaderboards, daily puzzles and duels may be reset, recomputed or removed while this is in beta. Scoring has already been fixed once, which means some older daily results were computed under a rule that was wrong. Treat a leaderboard position as interesting rather than as a record, and do not build anything on top of these numbers that you would be upset to lose.

Your account

  • Use an email address you control, and keep your password to yourself. Anything done from your account is treated as done by you.
  • A username is 3–20 characters of letters, numbers, hyphens and underscores, must not collide with an existing one by case alone, and can be changed once every 30 days — the first one you set is free.
  • A username is public. Do not pick one that impersonates somebody, or that you would not want on a leaderboard next to strangers. Abusive or impersonating names may be released without notice.
  • You can delete your account yourself at any time, from Settings. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what goes and what stays.

Do not abuse it

There is one small database behind this and no budget. Please do not:

  • script, automate or bot your way through runs, the daily or a duel — a leaderboard of scripts is a leaderboard of nothing;
  • hammer the API, scrape it in bulk, or run load against it;
  • probe for a way to read other people’s data, or to write scores that are not yours. If you find one, the useful thing to do is tell me — see below;
  • make accounts in bulk, or use one to get around a limit that applies to another;
  • hold yourself out as connected to this site when you are not.

Accounts doing any of that may be removed, and results that were obviously not played may be deleted from a board. There is no appeals process; there is an email address.
