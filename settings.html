Danger zone

Permanent

Deleting your account takes it off this site entirely. There is no grace period and nothing is archived.

If you delete your account:

  • every score you have saved is deleted, and so is every Zetamac Daily attempt — you come off every daily leaderboard, including past days.
  • you leave every clan you are in. A clan you own is handed to its longest-standing remaining member; if you were its only member, the clan is deleted with you.
  • duels you created are deleted, along with both sides' results. Duels you only played in stay, and you show on them as a deleted account.
  • your username is released, and anybody else can claim it afterward.
  • this cannot be undone. There is no way to restore the account or anything above, and signing up again starts from nothing.
